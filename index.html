Research activity · 2026

Two-month research internship in Greece

28.08–04.11.2026 · Kastoria, Greece

From 28 August to 4 November 2026, I completed a two-month research internship at the Department of Economics, University of Western Macedonia, in Kastoria, Greece, as part of the STER programme supported by the Polish National Agency for Academic Exchange (NAWA).

Research stay

The internship took place under the academic supervision of Professor Nicholas Tsounis. During the stay, I worked on planned research tasks and participated in consultations on econometric modelling, methodological choices and the interpretation of empirical results.

Ongoing cooperation

Our research cooperation continues remotely after the internship. We are currently working on the refinement of an econometric model, with the aim of developing a joint scientific publication.

Academic and cultural visits

The stay also provided an opportunity to visit other Greek universities. In Thessaloniki, my itinerary included Aristotle University and the historic building of its Faculty of Philosophy. In Athens, it included the National Technical University of Athens, the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, and the Athens University of Economics and Business.

Exploring Greek heritage

Beyond the academic programme, I had the opportunity to explore sites of exceptional historical significance, including the Ancient Agora of Athens and the Acropolis.

The internship was a valuable opportunity to advance my research and establish a basis for continued international academic collaboration.
